**TabPFN** foundation models are purpose-built for structured data - delivering classification, regression, and forecasting with zero-shot accuracy in seconds. Each model is optimized for different applications, from research exploration to large-scale production systems.

TabPFN-2.5 is the upcoming generation of our tabular foundation models, designed to push state-of-the-art zero-shot performance across classification and regression tasks. Detailed documentation and release notes will be available soon.
