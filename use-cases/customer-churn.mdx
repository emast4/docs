## Why Growth Teams Choose TabPFN

### Turn Data into Retention

Every customer interaction reveals retention insights. TabPFN extracts churn risk scores from usage patterns, support tickets, and engagement data - complete with risk factors and intervention recommendations.

### Proactive Customer Success

Stop losing customers to reactive support. TabPFN identifies at-risk customers weeks before they churn, ensuring your success team can intervene with targeted retention campaigns.

TabPFN automatically analyzes complex usage patterns and customer behavior to predict churn risk. This helps customer success teams intervene before customers leave.
</Accordion>
