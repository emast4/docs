## How It Works

Predict customer churn using behavioral and engagement data. TabPFN analyzes usage patterns, feature adoption metrics, support interactions, and billing history to identify churn risk through zero-shot inference on customer lifecycle data.

The following table summarizes key signals leveraged when building customer churn prediction models. Integrating behavioral and health signals enables TabPFN to capture complex churn patterns, supporting data-driven retention strategies with quantified risk scores.

| Time-to-value metrics | Training progress     |

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

<Accordion title="How does TabPFN's attention mechanism identify churn signals?">
  TabPFN's alternating row-feature attention models complex dependencies between usage patterns, engagement metrics, and customer health indicators, producing interpretable feature importance scores for churn risk analysis.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can TabPFN handle sparse customer behavioral data?">

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What deployment options support real-time customer scoring?">
